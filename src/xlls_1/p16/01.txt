原：See T. J. Cadoux, JHS68 (1948)70-123, for earlier archons and for full references to those listed here between 500 and 480. A complete list of the archons from 479/8 B.C. to 403/2 B.C. is preserved in Diodorus, Books 11-14; other documen-tation for the archons holding office in this latter period is supplied by R. Meiggs and A. Andrewes in Hill, Sources2397-401. Only the year in which archons entered office is given here; it must be remembered that they held office from summer to summer, i.e. that the archonlistedunder500was in office from summer 500 to summer 499,etc.

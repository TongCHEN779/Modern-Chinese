原：1 The fragments were found on the site of the old acropolis of Mytilene. Potamon sonof Lesbonax was an orator of great repute in Mytilene and had benefitted his city in a great many ways in the last half of the century. The grateful city erected a huge monument in his honor, its base containing a record of his activities on behalf of the people and the city. Among the preserved documents from that monument are the following: a letter of Caesar soon after Pharsalus, transmitted to Mytilene through the envoys Potamon and his companions, assuring the city of his good intentions (col. a); the ve mutilated conclusion of a letter written by some unknown Roman official, perhaps Caesar again (col. b, lines1-5); the present letter (col. b, lines 6–36); two decrees of the senate and a treaty with Mytilene (col. b, lines 36–43, and cols. c-d), for which see below, no. 97.

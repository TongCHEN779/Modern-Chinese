    In the production of this volume I have benefited from the assistance and criticism of the Series Editors, E. Badian and R.K.Sherk. In addition the whole work has been greatly improved by the advice of M.F. McGregor. I owe a special debt to my research assistant, Brian Lavelle, and to Barbara Parker, who typed the whole, very difficult text. Much of the cost of their labour was offset by a research grant from the University of British Columbia.

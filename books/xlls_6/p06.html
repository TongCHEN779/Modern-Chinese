    Spelling

    Most of the personal and place names are transliterated directly. However, the names of Greek and Roman authors as well as individuals are given in their familiar English or Latin spelling, and certain place names, more familiar to readers in a Latin spelling, are retained in that spelling, regardless of the language of the document.Latin names will regularly appear in their Latin spelling for the same reason, but the more unusual Greek names will be transliterated. To many Aetolia' is more familiar than‘Aitolia', while 'Cibyra' would be no more enlightening than 'Kibyra'. Still, I have not followed a rigid set of rules.
